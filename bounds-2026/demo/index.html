Tours and counts computed October 1-2, 2026 (Agent Team). Crossings count unordered pairs of moves whose open segments intersect properly. A shared endpoint does not count; several pairs at one point count separately. Turn = a square where the tour changes direction.

Paper: J. J. Besa, T. Johnson, N. Mamano, M. C. Osegueda, Taming the Knight's Tour: Minimizing Turns and Crossings (arXiv:1904.02824). The 2019 demo was made by Juan Jose Besa Vidal, Timothy Johnson, Nil Mamano and Martha Osegueda. Research record of the 2026 bounds: bounds-2026.
